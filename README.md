## ✨ Features

- **🎹 Key Selector** – Choose the concert key of your YouTube backing track (all 12 keys, major & minor).
- **🔄 Automatic Transposition** – Calculates +1 whole step up for B♭ flugelhorn – instantly visible.
- **🎼 3 Scale Types** – Minor Pentatonic, Blues Scale, and Major Scale as switchable tabs.
- **🎺 Note Cards with Valve Display** – Each scale note shown as a card with a graphical 3-valve indicator (pressed valves glow).
- **📋 Valve Reference Table** – Complete chart of all 12 notes with fingerings.
- **🌍 Key Overview** – All keys at a glance with pentatonic notes and valve combos.
- **📱 Responsive Design** – Dark theme, optimized for desktop & mobile.

## 📖 How to Use

### 1. Select Key

Choose the **concert key** of your backing track from the dropdown (e.g. *C Major / A Minor*).

### 2. View Transposition

Right below you'll see the **transposed key** for your B♭ flugelhorn (+1 whole step up).

> **Example:** C Major → D Major · A Minor → B Minor

### 3. Pick a Scale

Click one of the scale tabs:

| Tab | Description |
|-----|-------------|
| **Minor Pentatonic** | 5 notes – ideal for blues & rock solos |
| **Blues Scale** | 6 notes – minor pentatonic + flat fifth (b5) |
| **Major Scale** | 7 notes – complete major scale |

### 4. Read Notes & Fingerings

Each note is displayed as a card with:

- **Note name** (German notation: H, B, Es …)
- **Scale degree** (root, minor third, perfect fourth …)
- **Graphical valve display** – glowing circles = pressed valves
- **Valve combination** as text (e.g. `1-2`, `2-3`, `0`)

### 5. Use the Overview

At the bottom you'll find an **overview of all keys** – click any card to jump directly to that key.
